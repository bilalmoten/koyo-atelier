A little guidance before you begin

Choose notes for Bottle 1, plan your amounts, then weigh them into your bottle. Bottle 2 can be a variation or a completely different scent.

Use grams on the scale. Aim for 10 g per bottle ≈ 10 mL ≈ 300 drops. Volume and drops are approximate.

Descriptions are scent impressions. Your nose decides what you like; top, heart and base categories are guides, not a quality score.
