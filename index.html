🌿 Master Perfumer Blending Rules (10ml Bottle)

1. The 30–30–40 Golden Ratio

For a balanced 60-drop formula: aim for approx. 15 drops Top (fresh opening), 20 drops Heart (body & character), and 25 drops Base (warm lasting trail).

2. Musks = All-Day Tenacity

Always include ethical musks (White Musk, Galaxolide, Ethylene Brassylate). They act as fixatives so your fragrance lingers beautifully on skin.

3. Harmonic Pairings

Pineapple pairs with Leather & Musks; Lotus pairs with White Floral & Citrus; Rose Honey pairs with Marshmallow & Tobacco.
